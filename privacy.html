Helm Landfall — Privacy Policy

Effective 6 October 2026. Applies to the Helm Landfall app on iOS, Android and the web.

Helm Landfall is a habit and accountability app run by Joel Denomy, an individual developer. It is in a closed test. This page explains what the app stores, where, and how to remove it.

Without an account

You can use Helm Landfall without signing in. Your Duties, Log, Voyage and settings then stay on your device only. Nothing is sent to us.

With an account

If you sign in, the following is stored on our backend so it can sync between your devices and with your Crew:

  • Account: your email address and a password (stored hashed by our auth provider).
  • Profile: your sailor name, ship's name, chosen ship, timezone, rank and, if you choose one, a portrait photo you pick from your library.
  • Your Log: the Duties you set and the entries you record (for example amounts, units and times), your Voyage route and progress, and in-app rewards.
  • Crew and Family Ships (optional): membership, roles, Crew Challenges, short signals or messages (up to 180 characters) you send to crewmates, and Family Orders.

We do not collect your location, contacts, health data, advertising identifiers or device analytics. The app has no ads, no tracking and no third-party analytics. We never sell or share your data for advertising.

Who can see it

Your Log is private to you. If you join a Crew, crewmates see your name, ship, rank and progress, and your Duties only if you switch on sharing. In a Family Ship, the guardian sees the junior's Orders and progress.

Service providers

Data is stored with Supabase (database and sign-in), hosted in Sydney, Australia. The web version is served by GitHub Pages. Apple and Google deliver the native apps and may process crash reports under their own policies. Sign-in emails are sent by Supabase.

Permissions

  • Photos: only when you choose a portrait. No camera or microphone access.
  • Notifications: only if you set a reminder. Reminders are scheduled on your device.

Children

Helm Landfall is intended for people aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

Deleting your Helm Landfall account and data

We keep your data while your account exists. To delete it:

  • Delete your account: in Helm Landfall open Ship's Office → Account & crew service, type DELETE and choose Delete my Landfall account. This immediately and permanently removes your account, email address, profile and portrait, Log, signals, Crew and Family memberships from our backend. Command of a Crew passes to another member. Nothing is kept on our servers afterwards; the copy of your Log on your own device stays until you erase it.
  • Remove only your Log from the service: Ship's Office → Account & crew service → Remove my Log from the service deletes your synced Log without deleting your account. You can also remove your portrait under Sailor & Watch clock.
  • Erase this device: Ship's Office → Your Log's safekeeping exports or erases the Log stored on the device.
  • By email: write to joeldenomy@gmail.com from your account's address and we will delete your account and data within 30 days.

Security

Data is sent over HTTPS. Access is limited per account by database rules.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
